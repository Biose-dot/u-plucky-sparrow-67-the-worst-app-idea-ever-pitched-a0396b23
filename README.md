Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/c/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23).

SnoozeCoin mines crypto from your snooze button: your wasted mornings become tokens nobody will ever buy, the founder calls it "the future of sleep," and the leaderboard ranks the world's most committed oversleepers.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> SnoozeCoin mines crypto every time you hit snooze, turning wasted mornings into tokens nobody will ever buy — our founder calls it 'the future of sleep,' and the leaderboard ranks the world's most committed oversleepers.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
